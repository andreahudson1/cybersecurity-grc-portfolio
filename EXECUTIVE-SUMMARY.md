# Executive Summary

I am a cybersecurity-focused GRC professional with hands-on experience evaluating risk across vendor management, IAM governance, and vulnerability prioritization. My portfolio demonstrates a practical, business-aware approach to risk: I assess the issue, validate the evidence, prioritize the action, and communicate the decision clearly to stakeholders.

## Core Competencies
- Third-party/vendor risk assessment
- IAM access review and recertification
- Risk scoring and control mapping
- Vulnerability triage and remediation planning
- Executive-ready reporting and risk communication
- Audit and evidence support

## Portfolio Highlights

### 1. Healthcare Vendor Risk
The vendor risk example evaluates a PHI-handling vendor and demonstrates how I review intake information, validate questionnaire responses, map controls to frameworks, and make a recommendation based on the residual risk. The primary findings focused on missing annual penetration testing and unclear IR response time expectations, which led to a conditional approval recommendation.

### 2. IAM Access Review
The IAM example demonstrates how I review access entitlements, identify stale or unnecessary access, and evaluate whether access is still aligned to a valid business purpose. The project highlights how role changes, dormant accounts, and privileged access can create operational risk if left unmanaged.

### 3. Vulnerability Management
The vulnerability example shows how I prioritize technical findings using severity, exposure, and asset criticality. It focuses on how vulnerabilities move from technical findings to executive-level risk reporting and operational remediation.
