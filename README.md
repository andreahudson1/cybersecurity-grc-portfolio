The artifacts are designed to show how I assess risk, validate evidence, prioritize remediation, and communicate findings clearly to both technical and executive audiences.

## Risk Methodology
My approach to risk is grounded in evidence, business context, and prioritization.

1. Evaluate the business context and criticality of the system or vendor.
2. Collect and validate available evidence (questionnaires, policies, attestations, scans, logs, entitlements).
3. Map control gaps to recognized frameworks and standards.
4. Rank risks using likelihood × impact and operational criticality.
5. Assign ownership, remediation steps, and realistic deadlines.
6. Communicate the outcome in a way that supports decision-making at both operational and executive levels.

The framework references used in this portfolio include:
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- NIST SP 800-53
- HIPAA Security Rule
- CVSS v3
